● How It Works

From leftover to lifeline, in 4 steps

1

📋 Report surplus

Book a pickup or list your leftover food in under a minute — tell us what, how much and where.

2

🚛 Fast pickup

Our crew arrives in your chosen slot with sealed food-grade containers, within the safe food window.

3

🔍 Check & segregate

Food is temperature-checked and sorted: edible → donation, inedible → compost or biogas.

4

📊 Get your report

You receive a receipt with kg diverted, meals served and CO₂ saved — ready to share.

● Why Wastex

Why outlets and NGOs choose Wastex

🥗

Donation first, always

Edible food goes to people before anything else. Composting and biogas handle only what genuinely cannot be eaten.

⚡

2–4 hour response

Surplus food has a short safe window, so our routes are built around service times — lunch, dinner, bakery close.

💸

Free for donors

Donation pickups cost nothing. Our compost and recycling partners fund the runs, so doing good stays free.

🧤

Trained, uniformed crew

Handlers trained in food-safe practice, with sealed containers and a documented chain of custody on every load.

📊

Proof, not promises

Weighed loads, delivery photos and monthly impact reports — your CSR numbers are auditable, not estimated.

📄

Compliance handled

Donation receipts, disposal certificates and waste paperwork generated automatically for your records.
