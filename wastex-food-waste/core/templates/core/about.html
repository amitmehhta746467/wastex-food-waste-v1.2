● About Us

Turning responsible waste disposal into a better future for our planet.

Wastex started with one simple observation: at the end of every banquet, buffet and bakery day, good food went into bins while people nearby went hungry. We built the missing link — fast, safe, documented collection.

🥗
Donation first, always

🎯 Our Mission

No edible food wasted

To make sure surplus food reaches people first — with collection fast enough, and handling safe enough, that donation becomes the easiest option for every kitchen.

🔭 Our Vision

Cities where landfill is the last resort

A city where every restaurant, hotel, hostel and event treats leftover food as a resource — shared, composted or turned into energy, never simply thrown away.

🤝 Our Promise

Measured, documented, on time

Every load weighed, every delivery photographed, every partner verified. If we say it was diverted, the paperwork proves it.

● Our Journey

25 years of experience, one food-first mission

2001

Our founding team starts in municipal waste collection and recycling.

2008

Composting partnerships begin — kitchen waste returns to farms as compost.

2016

Biogas routing added for cooked and oily waste from large kitchens.

2021

Wastex launches its food-first model: donation routing ahead of disposal.

2024

Live Food Listings open — donors list surplus, NGOs claim it the same day.

2026

480+ partners across 26 cities, with a public zero-landfill-by-2030 commitment.
