● How It Works

From leftover to lifeline, in 4 steps

1

📋 Report surplus

Book a pickup or list your leftover food in under a minute — tell us what, how much and where.

2

🚛 Fast pickup

Our crew arrives in your chosen slot with sealed food-grade containers, inside the safe food window.

3

🔍 Check & segregate

Food is temperature-checked and sorted: edible → donation, inedible → compost or biogas.

4

📊 Get your report

You receive a receipt with kg diverted, meals served and CO₂ saved — ready to share.

● Our Standards

The Wastex quality standard, on every pickup

🌡️

Temperature checked

Every cooked-food load is checked at collection. Anything outside the safe range is composted, never donated.

🔒

Sealed & labelled

Food-grade sealed containers, labelled with dish, quantity and cooking time — a clean chain of custody.

📍

GPS-tracked vehicles

Every vehicle is tracked from your door to the NGO, compost unit or biogas plant it delivers to.

✅

Verified partners

NGOs, kitchens, composters and recyclers on Wastex are verified before they receive a single load.

📄

Full paperwork

Donation receipts, destruction certificates and compliance documents generated for every collection.

📊

Measured impact

Monthly reports show kg diverted, meals served and CO₂ saved — measured, not estimated.
