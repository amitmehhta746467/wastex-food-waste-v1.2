● Quick Tips

6 food waste tips that actually work

🏷️

Label everything

Dish name + date on every container. Most waste is just forgotten food.

🔁

First in, first out

New stock goes behind old stock — the simplest rule in professional kitchens.

❄️

Freeze with a plan

Freeze surplus in serving-size portions, and put a "use this week" date on it.

📋

Track for one week

Weigh what you bin for 7 days. The top 3 items are where your savings hide.

🍛

Plan a "use-it-up" special

One weekly special built from surplus turns would-be waste into margin.

🤝

Donate the rest

Whatever is still good, list it on Wastex — collection is free and documented.
