● Before You List

Donor checklist — 3 quick checks

⏱️

Still in the safe window?

Cooked food within 2 hours at room temperature, or kept hot above 60°C / chilled below 5°C. If not, book a compost pickup instead.

📦

Sealed & labelled?

Pack in clean, sealed containers and note the dish name and cooking time — it helps kitchens plan serving.

⚖️

Quantity roughly right?

An honest estimate in kg and servings means the right-sized NGO claims it — no wasted trips, no wasted food.

● For NGOs & Kitchens

Claiming food for your kitchen

Verified NGOs, shelters and community kitchens claim listings free of charge. Add your organisation name when you claim, then collect before the pickup-by time shown on the listing — the donor's contact number is shared with you the moment your claim succeeds.

✓
No fees, ever

Claiming and collecting donated food is always free for registered charities.

🌡️
Checked at handover

Donors follow the checklist above; check temperature on arrival and compost anything doubtful.
